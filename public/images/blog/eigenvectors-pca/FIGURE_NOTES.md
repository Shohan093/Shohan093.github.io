# Figure notes: Eigenvectors and PCA

Generated by `python-script/generate_eigenvectors_pca_figures.py`.

## eigenvector-transformation.svg

**Caption:** A symmetric 4×4 matrix and its four distinct eigenvalues, with
numerical residuals verifying Avᵢ = λᵢvᵢ.

**Alt text:** A heatmap of a symmetric four-by-four matrix beside a chart of
its four eigenvalues and near-zero eigenpair residual norms.

The matrix is constructed as A = QΛQᵀ with eigenvalues 7, 4, 2, and 0.5.
It is a general linear transformation, not a covariance matrix.

## covariance-geometry.svg

**Caption:** A six-feature synthetic dataset shown in its first two principal
coordinates, with a covariance ellipse whose semi-axes scale with √λᵢ.

**Alt text:** A cloud of observations in principal-component coordinates,
surrounded by a dashed ellipse aligned with the principal axes.

The figure is in PC-score space, not a pair of raw-feature axes.

## pca-projection.svg

**Caption:** One-component PCA retains PC1 and discards the orthogonal residual.

**Alt text:** Selected observations in the PC1–PC2 plane connect by dashed
segments to their projections on the PC1 axis.

Important: the displayed vertical residual is only the PC2 part. The reported
mean squared residual norm is calculated in all six original dimensions.

## explained-variance.svg

**Caption:** Individual and cumulative explained variance across six
principal components.

**Alt text:** A bar chart of per-component explained variance with a cumulative
line and a 90% reference level.

evidence about a real population.
